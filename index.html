The letter

My Beb,

Sometimes I still can't believe that it's already been six years since two people who met on Bumble somehow became us.

If someone asked me what caught my attention about you in the beginning, I'd say your smile.

There's something about your smile that, honestly, lightens my world. You also have that magaan na vibe, the kind of energy that makes people feel comfortable around you and makes everyone want to vibe with you.

But as I got to know you more, I realized that your smile wasn't the only thing that made me fall in love with you.

It's the way you're sweet whenever you're with me.

You might look strong in front of everyone else, but there's another version of you that comes out when you're in the mood, when you're happy, and especially when you're deeply in love. That side of you overwhelms me sometimes.

And I feel lucky because I feel like I'm one of the few people who gets to see that side of you.

My Love. My Mal. My Beb.

When I think about our six years together, I don't think of just one memory.

I think about every trip we've taken. Every staycation we've had. Every little escape we've shared.

Those moments are unforgettable to me because they're like little samples of the life we've always imagined having together.

A life where we can travel. A life where we can rest together. A life where we can wake up beside each other and not have to worry about when we have to go home. A life where we have the freedom to enjoy the things we've worked so hard for.

And I know we're not there yet.

Right now, we're still going through struggles—especially financially. We have debts, responsibilities, and so many things we're trying to figure out.

But I still believe in the future we've talked about.

I believe that someday, we'll look back at this difficult season and say, “We made it.”

And I hope that day comes soon.

I also want to say I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for the times when I wasn't strong enough to help you with the struggles you're carrying.

I'm sorry for the times my ego got hurt and I became short-tempered instead of trying harder to understand what you were trying to tell me.

I'm sorry for my anger and for the moments when my emotions got the better of me.

I know I still have things to work on.

I'm still learning how to control my anger, how to communicate better, how to listen without immediately becoming defensive, and how to become a better partner for you.

I'm not perfect. But I want you to know that I'm trying. And I hope you can see that.

Thank you, Beb.

Thank you for every sacrifice you've made. Thank you for supporting me, guiding me, believing in my strength, and staying beside me even during the moments when things weren't easy.

Thank you for being my girlfriend, my best friend, and sometimes even my mother when I needed someone to take care of me.

Thank you for checking on me. Thank you for visiting me unexpectedly. Thank you for making sure I'm okay whenever something happens to me.

You have this way of making me feel that I'm not alone. And for that, I will always be grateful.

I thank God for your existence.

After six years, I still care about you so much. Sometimes, probably too much.

I know that caring about someone more than yourself can sometimes become unhealthy, and I'm learning to understand that too. But I hope you always know that the love I have for you is real.

You are one of the best people I've ever met.

And I don't regret any of our memories. Not the happy ones. Not the difficult ones. Not even the moments when we misunderstood each other.

Because every single one of them became part of our story.

I love you when things are easy. I love you when things are difficult. I love you when we're laughing. I love you even in the middle of misunderstandings. And I still choose you.

Beb, after six years, I want you to know that I don't really have a big wish in life.

More than anything, I just want us to be successful in life.

We have the same goal to make our parents proud and happy, to build the life we've always dreamed about, and to finally enjoy our relationship with peace, freedom, and less worry.

I hope I get hired this month. Not just because I want a job, but because I want to be able to support you when you need me.

I want to be able to visit you easily. I want us to travel more. I want more staycations. I want more places that we can call our little escape.

I want us to have those moments where we can simply breathe, relax, forget about work for a while, and remind ourselves why we're working so hard in the first place.

I want to build that life with you.

Not a perfect life. Just our life. A peaceful one. A happy one.

A life where we can finally say, “We made it together.”

So, my Beb... Happy 6th Anniversary.

Thank you for six years of choosing me. Thank you for loving me. Thank you for staying. And thank you for being you.

If I could go back to August 23, 2020 and meet you all over again, knowing everything we would go through...

I would still swipe right.

I would still choose you. And I would still want our story to be ours.

Happy 6th Anniversary, Mahal.

Here's to the six years we've already lived... and to all the years we haven't written yet.

I love you. Always.

— Your Zion 🤍
